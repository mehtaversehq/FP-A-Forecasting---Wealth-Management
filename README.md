### Wealth Management Financial Planning & Analysis — Crescent Gulf Bank

Developed an end-to-end Excel-based FP&A model for a fictional Dubai-oriented wealth management division managing AED 571 million in assets.

The project covers historical financial performance, AUM decomposition, net new money analysis, product-level revenue, operating expenses, management P&L, budget variance analysis, client segmentation and relationship manager performance.

Constructed driver-based quarterly forecasts and Bear, Base and Bull scenarios for FY2026. Evaluated frozen forecasts against previously concealed synthetic outcomes and compared forecast accuracy with a historical run-rate benchmark.

Designed an executive management dashboard to communicate financial performance, growth drivers, profitability risks and forecast reliability.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, financial modeling, scenario analysis and data visualization.

**Data:** Synthetic financial dataset representing a fictional wealth management business.
